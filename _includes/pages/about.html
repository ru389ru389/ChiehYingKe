Biography

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Radar Laboratory, Department of Atmospheric Sciences, National Central University, Taiwan, specializing in radar meteorology. My research covers weather radar field observations, in-depth analysis of mesoscale severe weather, numerical model simulations, and advanced data assimilation techniques.

My current work focuses on thermodynamic structures and the benefits of radar data assimilation for cloud microphysics. I am also looking forward to investigating climate change, with a particular focus on the dynamics of the Arctic climate.

{% else %}

個人簡介

我是國立中央大學大氣科學系雷達實驗室的博士後研究員，專長為雷達氣象。 研究涵蓋氣象雷達野外觀測、中尺度劇烈天氣的深入分析、數值模式模擬，以及進階的資料同化技術。

目前的研究聚焦於熱動力結構的探討，以及雷達資料同化對雲微物理過程的影響。 未來也將投入氣候變遷研究，特別是北極氣候的動力過程。
